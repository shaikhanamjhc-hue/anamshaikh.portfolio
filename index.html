01 · Bags in the Wild

A spec content strategy for an indie travel-bag D2C brand, built to close a real gap in their Instagram presence.

SOCIAL STRATEGY

What's working

Strong product photography and founder-led storytelling carry real credibility.

The gap

The feed leans almost entirely on static studio shots — little real customer content showing the product in use.

The missed format

Reels are underused, even though travel-format short video consistently outperforms static posts for reach.

Pillar: "Bags in the Wild"

A recurring UGC and reels series showing real customers using their bags mid-travel — airports, road trips, hostels. People decide on travel gear by how it looks in use, not how it looks on a studio shelf. This gives the brand a repeatable, low-cost content engine instead of one-off product shoots.

02 · Nykaa — Instagram Audit

An independent spec audit of Nykaa's Instagram content mix, with five concrete content ideas built to close the gap.

CONTENT AUDIT

What's working

Polished, education-first tutorials and dermatologist/MUA collaborations build real trust with the audience.

The gap

Content is consistently high-production but rarely fast — few quick, native-feeling "get ready in 60 seconds" formats that trend audio rewards.

The missed moment

Shade-matching and "which product is right for me" is a huge pain point that's addressed in blog content, but barely shows up as scroll-stopping Reels.

Idea: "Shade Match, Not Guesswork"

A weekly Reels format solving the single biggest hesitation in beauty e-commerce — not knowing if a shade or product will actually suit you — using fast, native-feeling video instead of another polished tutorial.

01
"3 Shades, 1 Face" Reels

Split-screen reel testing 3 shades of one product on real skin tones in under 15 seconds — built for save-and-compare behavior.

02
"Ask a Dermat" Carousel Series

Weekly carousel answering one real customer question, sourced directly from Nykaa's own comments and reviews.

03
60-Second Get-Ready-With-Me

Trend-audio-led GRWM using only Nykaa products, timed to under a minute for full completion rate.

04
"Return Reasons" Honesty Reel

A transparency-format video addressing the top reason a product gets returned — builds trust precisely because it's unusual for a retailer to say.

05
UGC Restock Countdown

Story series counting down real customer restock requests — turns demand signals into content and urgency at the same time.
